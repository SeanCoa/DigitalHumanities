Compare geocoded coordinates side by side

Upload the CSV produced by the multi-source geocoder. For every Born, Died, and Education location, this shows every source's coordinate on a map alongside the raw values, so you can pick the correct one (or drop a custom pin) instead of trusting the automated aggregate blindly.

  1. Load the geocoded CSV (the one with *_Aggregate_* and per-source columns).
  2. Step through locations with ←/→, or jump straight to the risky ones with Next unresolved (prioritizes "no consensus" rows, then "partial agreement").
  3. Click a marker on the map (or a card on the right) to choose that source's coordinate as final. Click elsewhere on the map to drop a custom pin if none of the sources are right.
  4. Save progress anytime to a JSON file you can reload later; Download corrected CSV when done.

Nothing is uploaded anywhere — this all runs locally in your browser. The map tiles come from OpenStreetMap, so you'll need an internet connection while reviewing.
